## About the Lab
{: .center-heading }

The **Building Resilient Infrastructure and Adaptive Network Systems (BRIANS) Lab** develops data-driven, simulation-based, and governance-aware tools to understand how transportation and infrastructure systems perform under climate extremes, natural hazards, and operational disruptions.

## Research Thrusts
{: .center-heading }

<div class="research-thrust-layout">
  <div class="cards">
    <article>
      <h3>Climate and Natural Hazard Risk</h3>
      <p>We study how extreme heat, flooding, wildfire, and other hazards affect transportation and infrastructure systems.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <h3>AI-Enabled Infrastructure Decision Support</h3>
      <p>We develop AI, simulation, and optimization tools to support planning, operations, maintenance, and resilience decisions.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <h3>Human-Centered Resilience and Governance</h3>
      <p>We examine how institutions, households, agencies, and communities make decisions before, during, and after hazard events.</p>
    </article>
